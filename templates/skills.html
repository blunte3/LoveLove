Cognitive Reframing

Cognitive Reframing helps you identify unhelpful thoughts and reinterpret them in a more balanced, realistic way to reduce stress or anxiety.

How to Practice:

  • Notice a negative thought or assumption.
  • Ask: “Is this 100% true? What evidence do I have?”
  • Reframe it into a more neutral or positive statement.
  • Reflect on how the new thought changes your mood.

Progressive Muscle Relaxation

This relaxation technique involves tensing and relaxing muscle groups one at a time to reduce physical tension and promote calm.

How to Practice:

  • Sit or lie comfortably in a quiet place.
  • Tense a muscle group (like your fists) for 5 seconds.
  • Slowly release and focus on the feeling of relaxation.
  • Move progressively through your body.
